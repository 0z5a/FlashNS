# Prospective C36 time-to-verified-accuracy validation

This protocol is registered before running five previously unused initialization seeds on GH200. It measures absolute time to verified physical accuracy under a fixed inspection policy. There is no paired baseline and no speedup claim. Development seeds 361901/361902 and all unknown MN5 outcomes are excluded.

The authoritative machine-readable specification is [protocol-formal-tta-20261021.json](protocol-formal-tta-20261021.json). Seeds are 1212382065, 392322075, 422010988, 1127789372 and 949592700, generated once by the documented SHA256 rule. The independent common holdout seed is 562507784. All five remain in the denominator, including failures and missing outputs; there are no replacements or automatic retries.

The original eta=1/32 problem, cached training inputs, calibration, FP64 50,692-parameter network, Adam4000 and dense SSBroyden equations, numerical tolerances and every physical gate are unchanged. Each new initialization is checked against fresh full dense parameter AD and a fixed 131-interior-point native coordinate-AD subset with all value families and all parameter-gradient entries. The subset is not a fresh full-coordinate-AD proof over all 4096 interior points. Earlier full-native GH200 qualification is retained with its original scope.

After Adam, inspect at the first completed optimizer boundary at or after 1000 active training seconds. The observer uses the original full stratified and uniform samples, seven diagnostic time slices, initial/boundary tests, pressure gauge, and 27 trajectories at two refinements. If it passes, stop. Otherwise continue the same optimizer state until the original 4000-active-second guard or natural optimizer stop, then perform the terminal inspection. At most two complete observations run. No observer runs concurrently with training. The unchanged inverse Hessian is temporarily copied to host memory to leave GPU capacity for observation, and restored exactly on a negative observation. Checks and checkpoint writes count toward active training; synchronous observation and spill/restore do not. A boundary guard may overshoot by the last indivisible optimizer/check operation; record the actual time.

The primary TTA starts at trainer module entry, before heavy imports, CUDA initialization, data/model loading and JIT. It ends only after a complete physical acceptance report has returned and its checkpoint, protocol and raw-output hashes have been verified. It includes all preceding failed inspections. Queueing, batch/module admission, archive creation and later independent CPU readback are excluded from TTA and reported separately. A pass remains provisional until independent readback succeeds. Active training time at the passing checkpoint is a secondary sampled upper bound: the true first physical crossing between inspections is unknown. Terminal failure means not reached under this inspection policy, not proof that no earlier transient crossing occurred.

Five independent Slurm jobs each request one GH200, 16 CPUs, 128 GiB host memory and at most four hours. The total reservation ceiling is 20 GPU-hours; actual allocation time is taken from Slurm accounting. The frozen worst-case plan is 900 seconds setup, 4000 active training, two observations capped at 4200 seconds each, and 900 seconds for archival, totaling 14200 seconds within 14400. A per-seed cold compiler/cache directory prevents cross-seed warm-cache sharing. Prepared jobs launch the frozen driver automatically, preserve full negative/partial outputs, join owned children, seal and reread archives, and exit immediately. No idle holder, reservation-only job, extension or Mac-dependent cleanup is permitted.

After each allocation ends, confirm absence from squeue, terminal sacct state for the job and steps, child exit, final owned GPU process snapshot and original lock release. CPU-only independent readback checks archive/member/source/protocol hashes, complete gradients, checkpoints and events, holdout regeneration, all physical metrics and trajectories, and TTA timestamps. Five audited successes qualify this particular fixed-policy cohort. Report each outcome plus median/range only for 5/5 success, and the Wilson 95% interval for the success fraction. With fewer than five successes, retain all censoring/infrastructure/invalid entries and do not publish a success-only cohort median. Slurm COMPLETED is not a scientific pass.

Control regressions exercise real tiny Adam/SSBroyden continuation after a failed inspection, immediate stopping after a passing inspection, clock accounting on observer failure, complete-denominator handling, archive preservation and early admission failure. These tests are explicitly CPU fixtures, not physical acceptance or GPU performance evidence. Full raw evidence and runtime packages remain on Arrhenius; local files hold only source, small reports and receipts.
